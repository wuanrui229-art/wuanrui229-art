# Anrui Wu

I study International Tourism Management at Macau University of Science and Technology. My projects explore AI product design, human–AI interaction and the use of explicit rules to make generated outputs easier to inspect and control.

I work across product requirements, interaction prototypes and AI-assisted implementation. For research projects, I aim to make the evaluation evidence as accessible as the demo.

## Selected projects

| Project | Focus | What you can inspect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MICEPlan-Lab](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/miceplan-lab) | Deterministic validation and LLM layout repair | Frozen experiments, source code, independent adjudication and offline reproduction |
| [MICECAD AI Booth Planning](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/micecad-ai-booth-planning) | Constraint-aware exhibition planning | Interactive workflow, editable booth objects, rule evidence and approval |
| [Smart Shopping AI](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/smart-shopping-agent) | Explainable shopping decisions | Conversational prototype, recommendation pipeline, comparisons and project documents |
| [Nexus AI Learning Companion](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/nexus-ai-learning-companion) | Learning from fragmented materials | Knowledge organization, contextual chat, practice and career-preparation prototype |
| [村小丫 · Cunxiaoya AI Travel](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/cunxiaoya-ai-travel) | AI-assisted rural-tourism planning | Team-project source, preference matching, route generation and fallback logic |
| [Campus Carpool · 校园拼车](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/campus-carpool) | Campus ride-sharing coordination | [Web demo](https://campus-carpool-ten.vercel.app), simulated trip coordination, chat and fare splitting, and product requirements |
| [The Unfinished Table](https://github.com/wuanrui229-art/unfinished-table) | AI-mediated discussion | Multi-perspective dialogue, sources and uncertainty in an interactive product |

MICEPlan-Lab is the research system; MICECAD is a separate product prototype. Each repository describes its own implementation, evidence and limits. Product prototypes use demonstration data where stated and should not be read as claims of production deployment.
